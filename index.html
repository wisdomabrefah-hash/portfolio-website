Re-platforming a complex vehicle import notification service for HMRC

The problem

HMRC's Notification of Vehicle Arrivals (NOVA) service was being re-platformed. Users must notify HMRC when bringing a vehicle into the UK — a task wrapped in tax legislation most users have no prior understanding of. Existing content mirrored legal language directly, causing comprehension failures and support escalations.

⬚ Add annotated NOVA journey map here.
Full flow with decision labels across all screens.
User flow: NOVA vehicle notification journey · [Replace with real asset]

My approach

As sole UX writer embedded in an Agile Scrum team, I ran discovery sessions with BAs and developers to understand business rules before writing a line of content. Legislation was a design constraint — not a writing brief.

Decision — why plain English

The original draft used legal phrasing verbatim. User testing showed people stopped at technical terms. I rewrote microcopy using GOV.UK tone principles, significantly reducing comprehension failures.

2Vehicle Details

6Confirmation

Outcome

Content live and iterated against real user research. Improved task completion rates for vehicle arrival notifications. Cross-team knowledge sharing reduced rework and delivery timelines.
